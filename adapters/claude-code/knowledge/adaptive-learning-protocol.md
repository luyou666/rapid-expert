# Adaptive Learning Protocol

Choose a 5, 7, 9, or 12 day plan.

- 5 days: low-risk, low-complexity, strong adjacent user experience, lightweight deliverable.
- 7 days: default for most business, product, job-search, consulting, or startup research tasks.
- 9 days: use when the domain needs extra policy, competitor, open-source, toolchain, or current-information research.
- 12 days: use for high-risk, regulated, highly technical, or zero-baseline users with heavy deliverables.

Always cover:

1. domain map and terminology
2. industry chain and business model
3. fresh source research and source grading
4. moat, competition, and reusable alternatives
5. real workflow and expert judgment frameworks
6. cases and risk detection
7. final practical task and evaluation
